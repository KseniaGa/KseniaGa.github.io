🌱 Games Intersectional

Games Intersectional (GI) is a community for marginalized people in games — students, researchers, and indie developers navigating academia and the game industry.

✉️ Contact

Where it started

GI began in 2023 at the University of Klagenfurt, when alumni of the Game Studies & Engineering program wanted to extend the sense of support and belonging they had found during their studies.

Today, GI connects people locally and internationally through roundtables, workshops, and community events focused on inclusive futures for games.

GI Poster

Why this community exists

Welcoming diverse voices into games and inclusivity require spaces where people feel safe, supported, and empowered to share their ideas and experiences.

Community

  • 🌱 Founded: December 2023
  • 👥 50+ people on our Discord (with a smaller active core)
  • 🎮 10+ events including roundtables, workshops, and gatherings
  • 🌍 Local community in Austria with international participation

What we do

Monthly meetups + community-led events that help people connect, share work, and learn from each other.

🗣 GIRT — Roundtables

Our monthly roundtables bring together students, researchers, and developers to discuss projects, challenges, and ideas around games, academia, and creative work.

  • sharing research + creative projects
  • career and industry discussions
  • community support and mentorship

🛠 Workshops

Skill-sharing sessions run by community members — from creative writing to coding basics and game-making tools.

📚 Discussions

Informal discussions about games related to inclusivity, accessibility, and representation.

Who it’s for
