🌱 Guild: Games Intersectional (GI)

A community for marginalized people in games — students, researchers, and indie developers navigating academia and the game industry.

💌 Send Guild Mail

📜 Guild Lore

Founded in December 2023 at the University of Klagenfurt. Alumni of the Game Studies & Engineering program wanted to extend the sense of support and belonging they had found during their studies.

Today, GI connects people locally and internationally through roundtables, workshops, and community events.
