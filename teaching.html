🎮 Representation & Configuration in Games

Game Studies & Engineering Master’s Program · Winter Semester 2024–2025 · ~25 students

course design game studies representation

A course I designed and taught exploring perspectives, identity, accessibility, and experimental game design in video games. Topics include player perspective, queer theory, accessibility, weird games, and representation in interactive media.

Students analyze games, discuss theory, and develop their own experimental game concepts.

  • Accessibility and disability in games
  • Queer theory, identity, and representation
  • Weird games, perspective, and experimental design

♻️ Modeling Sustainability in Board Games

Game Studies & Engineering Master’s Program · Summer Semester 2025 · 70+ students

co-teaching game design sustainability

Co-taught course with 70+ students exploring sustainability through tabletop game design. Students worked in teams and participated in two game jams to prototype sustainability-focused board games.

✍️ Scientific Writing for Games

Game Studies & Engineering Master’s Program · Summer Semester 2026 · ~10 students

academic writing research skills new course

A course focused on academic writing, research communication, and publishing in game studies and HCI. Students learn how to structure research papers, develop arguments, and present their work to academic audiences.
