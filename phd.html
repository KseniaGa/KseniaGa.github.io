🎮 Tell Your Story Through Games

A participatory game jam/workshop about migration, identity, and belonging — supporting people to create a small game inspired by their own experiences.

What it is

  • One-day creative workshop
  • Participants choose what they want to share (and what to keep private)
  • Translation + laptops/materials + food/travel support
  • Mentorship from game designers and researchers
